01 · STILLNESS

Be still

“Be still, and know that I am God.” Psalm 46:10

Stillness is not wasting time. It is making room to notice God's presence before the noise of the day takes over. You can bring your worries to Him without having every answer.

Today: Sit quietly for two minutes and breathe a simple prayer: “Lord, I am here.”

Begin practice →
02 · COURAGE

Walk with courage

“Be strong and of a good courage; fear not, nor be afraid.” Deuteronomy 31:6

Courage does not mean that fear disappears. It means you continue to do what is right while trusting that God goes with you through difficult conversations, new beginnings, and uncertain days.

Today: Take one honest, faithful step toward the thing you have been avoiding.

Read lesson →
03 · GIVING

Live generously

“God loveth a cheerful giver.” 2 Corinthians 9:7

Generosity is more than money. It can be patience, encouragement, hospitality, time, or forgiveness. What we have becomes a blessing when we hold it with an open heart.

Today: Give someone your full attention and encourage them with sincere words.
