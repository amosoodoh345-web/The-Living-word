What stays on your device

Reading history, saved verses, journal entries, seminar progress, reminders, growth check-ins, preferences and private testimony drafts are stored only in your browser or app on your device. We do not receive them.

Bible text

To load chapters that are not bundled, the app requests Scripture text from the third-party service bible-api.com. Your device’s IP address and the passage requested are visible to that service.

Community testimonies

If you choose to create an account and submit a testimony or comment, we store your email address (through Supabase authentication), your submission, and its moderation status. Submissions stay private until a moderator approves them. Approved testimonies and comments are visible to anyone. You can report approved testimonies.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own pending testimonies and comments in the app. To request removal of an approved submission or deletion of your account, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
